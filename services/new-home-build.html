How It Works

The Build Process

01
Initial Consultation

We meet to discuss your vision, budget, and timeline. You walk us through what you want — we listen and ask the right questions.

02
Design & Planning

We develop a customized floor plan and finalize selections. Permits and schedules are arranged before a single board goes up.

03
Construction

Our crew gets to work. You're updated regularly, and we manage every subcontractor to keep the project on time and on budget.

04
Quality Checks

At key milestones we walk through the progress with you. Issues are caught early — not at the final walkthrough.

05
Finishing Touches

From trim work to final fixtures, every detail is completed to the same standard as the first nail driven.

06
Final Walkthrough

We walk through the completed home with you to make sure every detail meets your expectations before you move in.
