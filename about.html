About Molitor Construction

Our Story

Local Experience. Quality Craftsmanship.

Molitor Construction LLC is a trusted general contractor serving south-central Wisconsin. We specialize in home building, custom decks & outdoor spaces, and remodeling projects designed to improve the way you live. Every project is built with quality workmanship and attention to detail from start to finish.

Based in Pardeeville, Wisconsin, we help homeowners bring their ideas to life with dependable construction services tailored to their goals. Whether you're building a new home, upgrading your outdoor living space, or remodeling an existing room, we focus on delivering results that last.

At Molitor Construction LLC, we believe that a good construction experience is built on honesty, integrity, and communication. We keep you informed every step of the way — from the first conversation to the final walkthrough.
